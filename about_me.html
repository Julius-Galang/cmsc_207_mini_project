About Me

Hi everyone, I am Julius Galang and these are some of the things about me.

As an Instructor in UPLB, here is my short bionote:

Engr. Julius Angelo Galang is an Instructor at the Department of Industrial Engineering University of the Philippines Los Baños with a Bachelor’s degree in Industrial Engineering from the same University. He is currently taking Master of Information Systems from the University of the Philippines Open University. He has held management roles in manufacturing industries as well as in research in the academe. He also served as Systems & Management Consultant for Proud Pinoy MSMEs.Currently, he engages in collaborative projects including the UP System wide project for the development of the Budget Utilization and Liquidation System Analytics (BULSA) and Personnel Unified Systems Outlook (PUSO).

I am also an aspiring Cultural Worker together with my fiancee.

Julius Angelo Galang currently serves as the Creative Operations Manager of Banua SaGana Kolab, an organization focusing on research and artistic collaborations on decolonization and food sovereignty. They have organized various events such a "Bukas na Hapag", "Rice is Life" and "Gulay Pa More Makiling" in collaboration with various organizations sharing the same advocacies.

Hobbies & Interests

Food Explorations

My Fiance and I have a great passion for food. I am a vegetarian while she if plant-based. We love to check out restaurants and food places to taste various recipes and ways of cooking. Of couse we also do our own experiments in cooking. I myself have a simple database of some recipes (not all) that we have tried.

I am a lacto-ovo sattvic vegetarian. I don't eat fish, meat chicken, but I eat eggs and dairy but I don't eat onion and garlic.

Filipino Martial Arts

I also happen to have developed an interest in studying FMA(Arnis/Kali/Eskrima) and have been doing self-study since 2024. Not very consistent lately but trying to gain momentum so as to make it more of a routine. Nevertheless I simply love practicing it and learning about its historical roots. I have a compilation of drills and combat simulations which are actually a repository of links to reels posted by Masters and Blackbets. This is one aspect of social media that I like a lot.

Poetry and Theatre

I am also into arts whether as an enthusiast or as a practicioner. I have been writing poems but I keep it to myself all compiled in google sheet. Perhaps I'll make a website where I can share them someday. I also do performance arts from time to time with my fiancee and have been in theatrical plays long before that.
